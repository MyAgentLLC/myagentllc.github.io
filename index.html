Questions, answered

What does the free audit actually give me?

A written report: where your business leaks calls, leads, and hours; which fixes pay back fastest; and what each fix costs to build — whether you hire us, hire someone else, or DIY. Delivered within 24 hours of our call.

Do I need any technical skill?

No. Done-for-you services are fully built and handed over running. The playbooks are written for non-technical owners — if you can follow a recipe, you can follow a playbook.

What if it doesn't work for my business?

The audit tells you that before you spend big — that's the point. If the audit finds nothing worth automating, you found out free instead of paying $1,500 to find out.

How fast is the turnaround?

Audits: 24 hours. Build sprints: typically 1–2 weeks depending on scope. You'll get a firm timeline before anything starts.

Can I just buy a playbook instead?

Yes — that's what the Gumroad store is for. Use code NOVA20 for 20% off during launch.
