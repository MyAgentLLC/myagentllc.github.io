Questions, answered

What does the AI receptionist do?

It answers calls day or night, including when you are with a customer or done for the day. You keep your current phone number and your phone company. Calls forward over. If you would rather keep answering yourselves, we can text missed callers instead. We do not promise those texts come from the number you already use.

Are calls recorded?

If calls are recorded or transcribed, the greeting says so up front.

Does it work after hours?

Yes. The receptionist can cover nights and weekends. Text-back, if you choose that instead, can go out after hours too. Nobody has to sit by the phone.

Will this replace my staff?

No. A helper takes repetitive busywork off the owner and the people you already have. It does not replace anyone.

Who is this for?

Local service businesses in Redding and Shasta County. We start with small care homes, assisted living, and nursing facilities, then plumbing, HVAC, and electrical. Another local business can email us and say what you do.

What is the free business review?

A free business review. The review looks at the repetitive work, including calls. Each helper is $497. That price is per helper, not for a pack of them.

What does a Build Sprint cost?

One helper, one price: $497. Email us for an invoice. You get one invoice for that one helper before work starts. Another helper is another sprint at the same per-helper price. A bigger workflow that ties several steps or tools together is quoted case by case. Website work is quoted separately.

Is there a monthly cost?

You pay once to build a helper, plus a monthly cost to keep it running. The AI receptionist is $147 a month. Missed-call text-back is $97 to set up and $69 a month. Other helpers are quoted, and you see the monthly amount before you pay. After setup, month-to-month teaching is optional: regular sessions so you and your staff learn how the AI works and can run and adjust the helpers yourselves. We can plan for 3 to 6 months, depending on how comfortable you are with computers. The aim is that you do not pay us for help forever.

What else can a helper do?

Booking and reminders. Follow-ups on quotes and estimates. Review requests. Sorting the inbox and drafting replies. Back-office help: bookkeeping records, payroll prep for you to review, supply orders for you to approve, files, and schedules. Each of those is its own helper. Website builds, makeovers, local search, and marketing help are quoted on their own. We do not promise rankings or a specific result.

What is Mia?

Mia is the chat on this website. A chat helper on your site is its own Build Sprint. It is not included with another helper. A website build or makeover is quoted separately.

Is there a phone number?

MyAgent does not list a public phone number. Email us. We reply within one business day. Your business keeps its own number.
