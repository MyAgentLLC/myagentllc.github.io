Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026.

Hi, I'm Nova, the assistant on this site. MyAgent is a sole proprietorship in California. This page says what this website collects and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • Forms. The contact form and the questionnaire ask for your name, business name, email, phone, website, and the answers you type. They open your email app. This website does not keep a copy of the form. The message is sent only when you choose Send in your email app, and email providers then process it. Please don't include medical records, resident details, passwords, payment-card numbers, or other sensitive information.
  • Chat. Chat conversations are sent to an AI service to generate replies. If you use the chat, the messages you type and a short recent conversation go to our chat service for that purpose. If you turn voice replies on, the reply text is also sent to a speech service so it can be played aloud. If you use the microphone, your browser may send audio to its speech-recognition service. Don't send medical or other sensitive personal information in chat.
  • Visit count. The homepage asks our chat service for a count of visits. That request includes ordinary connection data, such as an IP address. These pages don't set advertising cookies, and we don't sell that count.
  • Fonts. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Google can see your IP address when your browser requests them.
  • Payments. PayPal links leave this site. Card details are entered on PayPal's site. We don't receive your full card number through this website.

Text messages

If you check the optional SMS box on the questionnaire, you are asking MyAgent to text you about that request. The box is unchecked unless you check it, and you can send the form without it. Checking it does not authorize a business to text its customers. A missed call is not, by itself, permission to text the caller.

When a client business sends texts, that business is the sender and is responsible for having permission. Mobile numbers and opt-in records are not sold, and they are not shared with third parties for those parties' own marketing. Carriers and messaging providers receive what they need to deliver a message. Reply STOP to opt out.

How we use it

To answer you, do the work you asked for, keep records the law requires, and protect the service. We don't sell personal information. Hosting, email, the AI service, a speech service, Google Fonts, and PayPal process information as described above. There is no fixed retention schedule posted here. Email us if you want something deleted.

Your choices

Email us to ask what we have about you, to correct it, to ask us to delete it, or to stop emailing you. We won't treat you worse for asking. This page does not decide which privacy law applies to MyAgent.

Health information

This site, and ordinary email or chat, are not for medical records or resident information. We don't set up workflows that handle protected health information without the agreements and safeguards that requires. MyAgent is not an emergency or medical service.

Keeping it safe

We limit who can see information we hold. No system is perfect. If something goes wrong that the law says we must tell you about, we will.

Kids

This site is for businesses. It isn't meant for anyone under 16.

Changes

If this page changes, the date above changes with it.
