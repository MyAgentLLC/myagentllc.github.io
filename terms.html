I'm Nova. These are the plain-English terms for using this website and for working with MyAgent, a California sole proprietorship.

What we do

We build helpers for local businesses. A helper takes repetitive busywork off the owner and staff. It does not replace anyone. The usual first helper is an AI receptionist that answers calls day or night. If you would rather keep answering, we can set up text-back for missed callers. We do not promise those texts come from the phone number you already use. You keep that number and your phone company. Calls can forward over.

Other helpers can cover booking and reminders, quote and estimate follow-ups, review requests, inbox sorting and draft replies, and back-office work such as bookkeeping records, payroll prep for your review, supply orders for your approval, and files and schedules. A bigger workflow that ties several steps or tools together is quoted case by case. Website builds, makeovers, local search, and marketing help are quoted separately. We do not promise rankings or any particular result.

The free business review, for the first 25 local businesses, is a written look at that work. It is advice. It is not a promise of leads, revenue, rankings, or any particular result.

Pricing

Each helper is a Build Sprint. The price is $1,500 per helper, or $750 per helper for the first 25 local businesses. That price is for one helper. It is not a bundle of several helpers. You pay once to build it, plus a monthly cost to keep it running. The monthly amount is in the written agreement. It is not listed on this website.

A bigger workflow is quoted case by case, based on what it has to handle. Website work is quoted separately, also case by case. Phone, texting, and software fees from other providers are separate and billed as used unless a written agreement says otherwise.

After setup, month-to-month teaching is optional. Those are regular sessions so you and your staff can learn how the AI works and run and adjust your helpers yourselves. We can plan for 3 to 6 months, depending on how comfortable you are with computers. The aim is that you do not pay us for help forever.

What is included is the scope in the written agreement, set out before payment.
